Sigil Weave

Build a spell as a spatial structure. Place rune layers on the field, connect the geometry, survive optional Arcade Parry interruptions, then release the completed sigil.

1 · CHOOSEPick one of three guided recipes, or Free Weave.
2 · SELECTTap the rune named by the current step.
3 · ANCHORTap the glowing point on the 3D field. Order and geometry both matter.
4 · PARRYIf enabled, enemy magic can interrupt between layers. Cross the moving bolt.
5 · RELEASEWhen the structure closes, release it. Stability + charge become coherence.

Why this is different from Rune Trace: Trace tests how cleanly you draw one rune. Sigil Weave tests composition and spatial memory: which rune belongs in which layer, where it is anchored, and how the layers connect.

Arcade Parry is optional. BLOCK protects the structure, DEFLECT adds a little charge, REFLECT adds the most charge, and a MISS frays stability. Turn it off for a pure composition puzzle.

Mistakes are recoverable. The wrong rune or anchor lowers stability but does not advance the recipe. Correct the same step and continue.
