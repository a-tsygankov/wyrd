Word Duel

01

Read the spell. Shape the counter.

The original Wyrd language game: infer a partly hidden enemy spell, choose a reaction, then compose a legal 2–4 glyph counter under a Focus budget. The resolver explains every outcome.

Rune Lab

Rune Fencing

02

Recognize the threat before the stroke is finished.

The opponent writes a rune over time. Read its shape as early as possible and draw the rune that counters it. Early reads charge harder; a second rune can complete Empower, Reflect, or Siphon combos.

Watch stroke→Read→Draw counter→Combo
Main skillVisual reading + memory
PressureReal-time 60-second duel

Rune Parry

03

Make the best read from incomplete handwriting.

A faster information-game variant of Rune Lab. You glimpse only the opening of the enemy rune, commit a response within four seconds, then both runes are revealed and compared.

Glimpse→Infer→Commit→Reveal
Main skillInference under uncertainty
RoundOne read + one commitment

Original arcade

Volley

04

Magic ping-pong with an elemental wheel.

Return one persistent bolt over the gate. Hit the timing window, choose a colour that beats or matches the incoming element, and spend Focus on Ward or Smash as the rally accelerates.

Read colour→Time return→Accelerate
Main skillTiming + element choice
WinDrain 5 hearts

Original arcade

Quickdraw

05

Commit fast, charge hard, or spend Focus to ward.

Both mages choose once inside a three-second ring. Draw in the first second for a quick bonus, hold to grow an orb, or ward instead. When the ring closes, both choices collide.

Read orb→Draw / charge / ward→Clash
Main skillRisk vs information
InputOne-thumb hold/tap
Play Quickdraw →

Beam Clash

06

A rhythm tug-of-war inside a spell collision.

Two beams meet in a central knot. Push on the beat to move it toward the opponent; a perfect beat pushes farther, and the elemental advantage can double the force.

Choose colour→Hit beat→Push knot
Main skillRhythm + adaptation
WinFirst to 3 seals

Gate Tug

07

Quickdraw choices, but the battlefield remembers.

Use Quickdraw’s colour/charge/ward choice to push a shared gate along a five-step rail. The gate inherits the last push’s colour, so the next exchange must beat or match its current temper.

Draw→Resolve push→Change gate
Main skillTempo + board state
WinPush gate into enemy circle
Play Gate Tug →

Ward Rhythm

08

Defend a rhythm volley, then turn defense into attack.

Several elemental bolts arrive on a beat. Tap the colour that beats each bolt—or match it to absorb power—then use what you stored to throw back at the opponent’s ward.

Read sequence→Ward on beat→Throw

New POC

Rune Trace + Arcane Parry

09

Draw accurately to attack; physically intercept magic to defend.

Each exchange asks for a random offensive rune. Accuracy, form, direction and speed determine the cast. Then an opponent spell crosses the cinematic 3D arena and you Block, Deflect, or Reflect it with another stroke.

Trace→Cast→Read attack→Parry
Main skillStroke execution + reaction
FlowDynamic auto-advance
Play Trace + Parry →
Newest POC

Sigil Weave

10

Build magic as a spatial structure, not a single gesture.

Choose one of three guided recipes—or improvise in Free Weave—then place rune layers on six anchors. Geometry, order, stability and charge determine the final coherence. Optional Arcade Parry interrupts the construction.

Select rune→Anchor→Connect→Release
Main skillComposition + spatial memory
Variants3 recipes + sandbox
Play Sigil Weave →No Parry
