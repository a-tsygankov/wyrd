How the prototype scores you

  1. Rune Trace: Accuracy = shape match; Form = recognition confidence; Direction = correct stroke direction; Speed = execution tempo. These combine into overall cast quality and damage.
  2. Arcane Parry: Timing = how close your crossing was to the bolt; Angle = how steeply you crossed its path; Curve = how much the stroke bends; Quality combines those signals.
  3. Effects are deterministic: BLOCK prevents the incoming hit. DEFLECT prevents it and deals a small counter. REFLECT prevents it and returns a stronger counter. MISS causes damage.

Prototype note: PIERCE, REDIRECT and POWER are currently different tracing challenges, but all three offensive runes convert execution quality into direct damage. Their deeper spell-specific semantics are intentionally not simulated yet.
