How the dynamic duel works

  1. Dynamic duel is ON by default: after an exchange resolves, the next exchange starts automatically. Turn it off to restore the Next exchange button.
  2. Opponent behavior varies: aggressive spells are faster, patient spells give more read time, trickster spells use unusual lanes, and volatile spells pressure reaction speed.
  3. Suggestions are advisory: they explain a safer response, but BLOCK / DEFLECT / REFLECT still depend on the actual stroke you make.
